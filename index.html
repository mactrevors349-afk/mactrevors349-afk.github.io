Featured · 3D action RPG

Ariel — Heir of Thunder

Chapter I: The Spark of War

A third-person action RPG about the Greek gods that runs in the browser. You play Ariel, a high-school junior who learns he is the son of Zeus, and fight your way through Elysia to the God of War.

  • Souls-style combat: stamina, lock-on, dodge rolls, combos, spells and two-phase bosses
  • Six god powers, three from Zeus and three from Ares, such as Chain Lightning, Thunder Step and the Spear of Ares
  • Free flight with sky bolts and dive strikes, plus a Berserker God Mode with its own cutscene in which Zeus and Ares pour their power into Ariel
  • The world, music and sound effects are generated by code when the game loads
  • Static 3D sculpts are auto-rigged in the browser, and Mixamo motion capture (running, texting) is retargeted onto the rig
  • 151 voiced dialogue lines, recorded with neural voices by a Python tool that reads them straight from the code
  • Photo-scanned textures, cloth-simulated capes, film lighting for cutscenes, bloom and film grain
  • JavaScript
  • three.js r160
  • WebGL 2
  • Python
